Supplementary Movie 1. Lesion size and location. The first ten seconds compare 1,000-, 4,000- and 16,000-cell lesions at site 20 (top row) and site 25 (bottom row), with six matched 3D paths. The next 18 seconds reveal nine nested site-20 lesions, from 250 to 64,000 lost neurons, and all five Fig. 1f readouts. Each lesion size is a separate simulation. Points show means and ribbons the empirical central 95% simulation range across 100 realizations, with 20 gusts averaged for movement. Spared visual activity has a separate panel. The anatomy marks actual lesion masks.

Supplementary Movie 2. Neuronal responses to looming input, comparing intact, a 2,000-cell site-20 lesion and 100 replacements with original connections. Nine seconds of playback show 59–300 ms of the 300-ms trial at 30 fps. Purple marks input, blue host, teal replacement and orange motor activity, with an 8-ms display decay. Motor traces show the arithmetic mean of TTMn and DLMn population rates over the preceding 25 ms, matching the looming motor readout in the figures. Anatomy and rate axes share scale.

Supplementary Movie 3. Selecting cells to restore after a 2,000-cell site-20 lesion. Four matched ten-second paths compare no graft and 100 lost cells selected randomly, by projection priority or by motor-control priority. All replacements retain their original identities and available partners. Random selection chooses missing cells and differs from random wiring in Fig. 2. Colored anatomical points in the insets show the selected cells within the shared lesion. This comparison corresponds to Supplementary Fig. 4a.

Supplementary Movie 4. Replacement identity and wiring. Five Fig. 2 conditions are compared: no graft, original repair, random wiring, correct-type graft and non-target graft. Separate ten-second chapters show 100 and 2,000 intended replacements after the same 2,000-cell lesion, matching Fig. 2b. Each dose is a separate simulation. Correct-type and non-target grafts copy partners from surviving cells of the assigned type using the same integration rule. Tables show mean distance, wing-motor and looming-motor output across 100 realizations, normalized to matched intact. Only complete original repair reproduces intact exactly; random wiring and non-target identity remain impaired at full dose. All panels share one camera and scale.
